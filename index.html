How to Play & Controls

⌨️ Desktop & Camera Controls

  • W / A / S / D or Arrow Keys: Run & steer hero
  • Spacebar: Jump (bounce high on red mushrooms!)
  • Drag Mouse or C Key: Orbit 360° / Switch Selfie Camera
  • E Key: Talk to Crystal Guardian

🪞 See Yourself & Audio Features

  • 🎥 Cam Button: Switch between Close, Selfie Front, Action & Top views
  • 🪞 Mirror: Show or hide your Hero Mirror card
  • 🎧 Listening: Real VOA recordings. Read the question first, then press Play. Two plays per recording; tick 🐢 Slower if needed.

🏆 Scoring & Star Rating Rules

✨ Correct, 1st try: +100 pts
✨ Correct, 2nd try: +50 pts
❌ Wrong twice: 0 pts (answer shown)
💎 Crystal Coin: +10 pts
🎁 Hidden Chest: +100 pts
🌀 Level Clear: +300 pts

🎙️ Audio credits

All recordings and transcript extracts are from VOA Learning English, whose texts and MP3s are in the public domain. Series used: Let's Learn English (Levels 1 & 2) and Education Tips. Questions were written for this game.
